Our Story

A Legacy of Hospitality Excellence

Devdas J. Shetty

Founder • Director • Hospitality Entrepreneur

With over 25 years of dedication to the hospitality industry, Devdas J. Shetty has built a reputation rooted in quality, trust and an unwavering commitment to excellence. Starting his journey from humble beginnings, he rose through the ranks of the food and catering industry, driven by a passion for delivering exceptional dining experiences.

As a seasoned entrepreneur and business leader, Devdas has successfully established and managed multiple ventures across food & catering, hospitality management and business development. His vision for Elite Aroma's Café & Bistro was born from a deep desire to create a space where every guest feels valued, every meal tells a story and every visit becomes a cherished memory.

Under his leadership, Elite Aroma's represents the culmination of decades of expertise, combining traditional Indian warmth with contemporary culinary excellence. His philosophy is simple yet powerful: quality ingredients, skilled craftsmanship and genuine hospitality create unforgettable experiences.
